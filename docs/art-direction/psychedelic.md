# Art direction: psychedelic

## Intent
- Personality: An expansive poster: liquid colour, rhythm and shock while subject stays recognisable.
- Suitable for: music, biology, chemistry, festivals, consciousness
- Limits: legal, medical instructions, conservative finance

## Composition
- Grid: All-over composition: blobs, rings and type overlap; one quiet corner for CTA; poster panels with thick keylines.
- Allowed asymmetry: Rotated 3° on one word; off-centre blobs; crop can cut a shape.
- Scale: Display 68–120px against 16–18px body; ≥4x jump.
- Empty space: One quiet corner reserved for CTA; otherwise dense.
- Alignment: Overlapping, not centred; poster fold allowed.
- Density: High visual density; body stays flat colour, never inside gradient.

## Typography
- display: Bungee Shade 400 (bundled)
- reading: Space Grotesk 400 (bundled)
- label: Space Grotesk 500 (bundled)
- figure: Bungee Shade 400 (bundled)
- annotation: Space Grotesk 400 (bundled)
- Sizes by format: web=Display 68–120px uppercase, body 16–18px, labels 11px; slide=Display 96–140px, body 20–24px, labels 12–16px; gif=Display ≥40px at 640×360, body ≥18px, labels ≥12px
- Leading: Display .88, body 1.6, labels 1.2.
- Reading width: Body 50–60ch on web; 38–46ch on slides; 24–32ch on GIF.
- Display usage: Already decorative; no second display face; one word curved/rotated 3°; body flat colour.
- Explicit fallback: Approved glyph fallbacks: Archivo Black/EB Garamond/Space Mono (src/narrative/fonts.ts).

## Color
- Surfaces: #1B1B3A, #2A1A5E
- Text: #F7E8C8
- Accents: #C7166E, #FF6B1A, #F5E13B, #00A6A6
- Semantic states: ok=#00A6A6, warn=#F5E13B, problem=#C7166E, neutral=#B9A7FF
- Contrast notes: Night ground; magenta/orange hot pair; acid single hit; gradients only inside blobs; type on flat colour.

## Materiality
- Borders: 0–4px; thick black keylines on poster panels; hard offset shadows magenta/orange allowed.
- Shadows: Hard offset in magenta/orange; no soft grey shadows.
- Grain: None specified; colour fields.
- Cutouts: Liquid blobs, concentric rings, sun with face, abstract cells/eyes.
- Depth: Blobs drift 20–40px over 9s; hover expands 4%; reduced motion freezes.
- Readability rule: Body flat colour; no illegible body; no strobing above 3 Hz; max three hot colours.

## Illustration
- Vocabulary: liquid blobs, concentric rings, sun with face, abstract cells, eyes, hard offset
- Entities: Entities as liquid blobs and rings; no stock psychedelic art or AI glow.
- Relations: Relation through overlap and colour temperature.
- Actions: Actions as sensory verbs and plain explanations; CTA in quiet corner.

## Motion
- Entries: Swirl 550ms, 18px rise, stagger 60ms.
- Transformations: Blobs drift 20–40px over 9s; hover expands 4%; reduced motion freezes blobs.
- Continuity: Page 800ms; blobs drift like heat.
- Rest during reading: Type stays readable; manchas fijas in reduced motion.

## Negative rules
- No soft shadows.
- No more than three hot colours.
- No illegible body copy.
- No strobing above 3 Hz.
- Do not distort paragraphs.

## Adaptation
- Web: All-over poster composition with liquid blobs, rings, type overlap, quiet CTA corner. (example: examples/showcase/psychedelic/index.html)
- Slides: Poster slides with thick keylines, hard offsets, one quiet CTA corner. (example: examples/agents/round-2026/psychedelic.html)
- GIF: Blobs drift 20–40px over 9s; reduced motion freezes; short transitions. (example: examples/gifs/showcase-psychedelic.gif)
